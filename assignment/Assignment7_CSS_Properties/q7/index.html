The Art of Typography

Good typography makes text easier to read and more pleasant to look at. This page shows how highlighted text, bold emphasis, and italic emphasis can be combined for clarity.

Typography involves choosing the right font family, weight, and size for the content. Spacing between lines and letters also matters a great deal, since it directly affects how comfortable a paragraph is to read over a long passage of text. Careful attention to these small details is what separates an average page from one that feels genuinely well designed.
