Inspiring Quotes

On Passion

— Steve Jobs

On Perseverance

Success is not final, failure is not fatal: it is the courage to continue that counts.

— Winston Churchill

On Dreams

The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams.

— Eleanor Roosevelt

Quotes inspire people because they capture powerful ideas in just a few words, offering wisdom and motivation drawn from the experiences of others. They remind us that challenges are common and that perseverance leads to growth.
